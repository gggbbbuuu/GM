ANT1 Player addon for Kodi
======================

About
-----
ANT1 live and on-demand broadcasts, Greece and Cyprus

Kodi Addon for http://www.antenna.gr and https://www.ant1live.com

This addon is not published nor endorsed by antenna.gr or ant1live.com

This addon offers content available in Greece and Cyprus


Features
--------
Greece (antenna.gr):
- Four live channels (ANT1, MAK, Comedy, Drama) with current program titles
- Full shows index with server-side categories (series, news & current
  affairs, entertainment, bulletins, sports) and complete per-show archives
- News and Lifestyle sections with server-side paging (video clips play,
  text articles are listed for information)
- Sports highlights from the Formula 1 and football hubs
- Latest clips from Web TV
- Radio: the soundis station bouquet (Rythmos 94.9, easy 97.2, ANT1RADIO
  and thematic streams), read live from the public station list

Cyprus (ant1live.com):
- Live channel
- News, entertainment, podcast, series and movie sections
- Full detail pages for movies and episodes: cover fanart, posters, plot,
  director, cast, year and genre

General:
- Region switching (Greece / Cyprus) via a dedicated selector line or the
  context menu
- Playback resolved through ResolveURL and the PluginsGR Ant1GR/Ant1CY
  resolvers
- Items gated behind ANT1+ show an info message instead of failing
- Bookmarks and a function cache that can be cleared from the settings


Artwork
-------
Section artwork drawn in the addon style (white on black, 256x256),
plus the original ANT1 icon set.
